Privacy / 隐私

Created and maintained by akashmahedy / 由 akashmahedy 创建和维护。

No telemetry or maintainer proxy. Your credential stays in macOS Keychain, a Windows per-user DPAPI-encrypted file, or a private Linux file (not encrypted by this plugin), and is sent directly to Tencent's official MCP endpoint. Codex and Tencent process your document requests under their own policies.

本插件没有遥测或开发者代理。凭据保存在 macOS 钥匙串、Windows 用户专属 DPAPI 加密文件，或 Linux 私有文件中（插件不加密该文件），仅发送给腾讯官方 MCP 服务。文档请求由 Codex 和腾讯处理，其平台政策适用。

Deleting local storage does not revoke a Tencent token. Revoke it at Tencent when needed. Do not send credentials or private documents in support requests. / 删除本地凭据不会在腾讯撤销令牌。必要时请在腾讯撤销，反馈时请勿提供凭据或私人文档。
